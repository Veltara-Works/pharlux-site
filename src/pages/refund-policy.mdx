# Refund & Cancellation Policy

_Last updated: [set at launch]_

We want you to buy Pharlux with confidence. This page explains exactly how refunds and cancellations work on our self-serve plans — **Team, Business, Scale, and the commercial license**. Custom / Enterprise agreements are governed by their own signed contract, not this page.

## 14-day money-back guarantee

**Your first purchase of any self-serve plan is covered by a 14-day money-back guarantee.** If Pharlux isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of that first payment and we'll refund it in full — no forms, no hoops.

- The guarantee applies to the **first purchase** of a plan, on both **monthly and annual** billing.
- When we issue the refund, your commercial license is **revoked** and Pharlux reverts to its free, open-source (AGPL-3.0) terms. You're welcome to keep running the community build.
- The guarantee is a one-time confidence window for getting started — it doesn't apply to renewals (see below).

To request a refund, email **hello@pharlux.com** from the address you purchased with, or use our [contact form](/contact?intent=licensing).

## After the first 14 days, and on renewals

Outside the 14-day window — and on any renewal — **payments are non-refundable**, but you're never locked in:

- **Cancel anytime.** Cancelling stops the next renewal — we don't charge you again.
- **Your license stays active until the end of your current billing period, then expires.** You keep full access to everything you've paid for until that date. For example, on a billing cycle from 14 June to 13 July, cancelling on 30 June leaves your access running until the end of 13 July.

You can cancel yourself at any time from the **billing portal** linked in your account and purchase emails, or by contacting us.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails (an expired card is the usual reason), we don't cut you off:

- We automatically retry the payment over roughly two weeks and email you to update your card.
- **Your license stays active the whole time we're retrying.**
- If it still can't be collected after the retry period, the subscription cancels and the license expires.

## Chargebacks

If something's wrong with a charge, **please contact us first** — a quick email is almost always faster than a bank dispute, and if you're inside the 14-day window you're entitled to a full refund anyway.

A chargeback (a payment reversal raised through your bank) results in the associated license being **revoked immediately**, and we may decline future purchases from the account. Where a chargeback is raised in error, contact us and we'll help sort it out.

## Your rights under law

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, or modifies any consumer guarantee, right, or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law or any other law that cannot lawfully be excluded. Where such a guarantee applies and cannot be excluded, our liability is limited to the extent permitted by that law. This policy operates **in addition to** those rights, not instead of them.
