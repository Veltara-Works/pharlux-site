# Compare Pharlux

**Last updated: 2026-06-30** · Applies to: Pharlux v1.1.3

Pharlux is a single 86 MiB binary that ingests OpenTelemetry metrics and logs, stores them as local Parquet, and serves SQL queries, dashboards, and alerting from one process on a single VPS. Most alternatives take the opposite shape: a strong tool for one signal, plus the separate components you assemble around it. These comparisons are first-party and honest — they name where each tool is genuinely good, and where Pharlux's one-binary, single-VPS design is the better fit for a small team running 1–10 services.

## How to think about the choice

A useful way to narrow it down:

- **You want one tool, not a stack.** If "observability" currently means running and correlating four or five components for a handful of services, Pharlux collapses that into one binary. Start with [Pharlux vs Grafana Loki](/compare/grafana-loki) or [vs Prometheus](/compare/prometheus).
- **You're evaluating other unified, self-hosted platforms.** [Pharlux vs SigNoz](/compare/signoz) (no ClickHouse to operate) and [Pharlux vs OpenObserve](/compare/openobserve) (scoped for the single VPS) are the closest head-to-heads.
- **You're priced out of, or uneasy with, SaaS.** [Pharlux vs Datadog](/compare/datadog) covers the cost and data-residency case for bringing observability back in-house.
- **You have a dedicated metrics workload.** [Pharlux vs VictoriaMetrics](/compare/victoriametrics) is the honest take on a specialised TSDB versus unified metrics + logs.

Every comparison links through to the same first-party evidence: the [benchmarks](/benchmarks) (250,000 metric points/sec sustained on a 4 vCPU / 8 GB VPS, reproducible from the source tree) and the [pricing](/pricing) (flat tiers, no per-host meter).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best self-hosted observability tool for a small team?

It depends on what you need. Most self-hosted options — Grafana Loki, Prometheus, VictoriaMetrics — cover one signal well and expect you to assemble and operate the rest. Pharlux is built for a small team on a single VPS that wants metrics and logs unified in one binary, with dashboards, SQL, and alerting included.

### Is Pharlux a self-hosted Datadog alternative?

For metrics and logs on infrastructure you control, yes. Datadog is SaaS-only and priced per host, with telemetry stored on Datadog's cloud. Pharlux runs on your own VPS for a flat monthly license and keeps all data local. Pharlux does not match Datadog's full breadth of integrations, RUM, and synthetics.

### How is Pharlux different from the Grafana LGTM stack?

The LGTM stack is Loki, Grafana, Tempo, and Mimir — separate components for logs, dashboards, traces, and metrics, plus Alertmanager and object storage. Pharlux replaces that with one 86 MiB binary for metrics and logs (traces in V1.1), with built-in dashboards and alerting and no object store to operate.
