# Self-hosted Datadog alternative

**Last updated: 2026-07-06** · Applies to: the Pharlux v1.2 line

Datadog is the most polished observability product money can buy. The operative word is *buy*: it is SaaS-only, priced per host plus per GB plus per million events, and every byte of your telemetry lives on Datadog's cloud. For a small team that wants its data on its own infrastructure and a bill that doesn't scale with every server it adds, Pharlux is the self-hosted alternative — one 86 MiB binary that ingests OpenTelemetry metrics and logs on a VPS you control, for a flat monthly license.

## Pharlux vs Datadog at a glance

| | Pharlux v1.2 | Datadog |
|---|---|---|
| **Deployment** | Self-hosted binary on your VPS | SaaS only — no self-hosted edition |
| **Where data lives** | Your VPS (local Parquet) | Datadog's cloud (region you select) |
| **Pricing model** | Flat monthly license | Per host + per GB + per million events |
| **Indicative cost at 250 hosts** | $899/mo (Scale, unlimited hosts) | ~$130k/yr (Infra + APM, before logs)* |
| **Signals** | Metrics + logs (traces on the roadmap) | Metrics, logs, traces, APM, RUM, synthetics, security |
| **Integrations** | OpenTelemetry-native | 900+ turnkey integrations |
| **Query language** | SQL across metrics + logs | Proprietary query + dashboards |
| **Lock-in** | AGPL-3.0 source, open formats | Proprietary agent, query, dashboards; no bulk export |

<p><small>* List prices, approximate, ~2026: Infrastructure ~$15–23/host/mo, APM ~$31–40/host/mo. Real bills run higher once log ingestion and custom-metric overages are added.</small></p>

## The pricing problem, in numbers

Datadog's bill is not one number — it is a stack of them. Infrastructure monitoring runs roughly $15–23 per host per month. APM adds about $31–40 per host. Logs are billed both on ingestion (per GB) and on indexing (per million events). Custom metrics carry per-host allowances with overages beyond them. Each line is reasonable on its own; together they compound, which is why Datadog bills routinely overshoot the estimate.

Put 250 hosts through Infrastructure plus APM alone and you are near **$130,000 a year** — before a single log line is indexed. Pharlux's Scale tier is **$899 a month, flat, for unlimited hosts**, because Pharlux does not bill per host at all. Host and retention figures on the commercial tiers are generous fair-use ceilings, not a meter. The number that grows your Datadog invoice — adding servers — does nothing to a Pharlux license.

## Your data, your infrastructure

With Datadog, telemetry leaves your premises the moment it is collected. It lands in Datadog's cloud, in whichever region you choose, and it stays there — you cannot move it between regions, and you do not hold the raw store. For teams under data-sovereignty rules, in air-gapped environments, or who simply prefer their observability data not sit in a third party's cloud, that is a hard stop.

Pharlux writes everything to local Parquet on your own VPS. Nothing egresses. The Scale tier adds explicit air-gapped and binary-redistribution rights for environments with no outbound internet at all. You can read the source — Pharlux Community is AGPL-3.0 — and the storage is open Parquet, not a proprietary store you cannot leave.

## What Datadog does that Pharlux does not

A fair comparison names this plainly: Datadog's breadth is real and Pharlux does not match it. 900+ integrations, real-user monitoring, synthetics, security monitoring, FedRAMP, and a fully-managed control plane you never operate — that is a category of product Pharlux is not trying to be. If you need that surface area and the budget supports it, Datadog earns its price.

Pharlux makes a narrower promise and keeps it: OpenTelemetry-native metrics and logs (traces on the roadmap), queried with SQL, on hardware you own, dogfooded in Veltara Works' own production stack. For the team that is using a fraction of Datadog's features and paying for all of them, that narrower promise is the upgrade.

## Moving from Datadog to Pharlux

Datadog speaks OpenTelemetry, so the exit ramp already exists:

1. Stand up Pharlux on a VPS with `sudo pharlux install` (see the [getting-started guide](https://github.com/Veltara-Works/pharlux/blob/main/docs/user/getting-started.md)).
2. Add a Pharlux exporter to your OpenTelemetry Collector on `:4318`, alongside the Datadog one.
3. Rebuild the dashboards and alerts you actually use as Pharlux SQL, and watch your host-based bill stop growing.
4. Wind Datadog down once Pharlux covers what your team relies on.

## Is Pharlux the right fit?

If you depend on Datadog's full breadth — synthetics, RUM, security, hundreds of managed integrations across a large fleet — Pharlux is not that product, and this page won't pretend otherwise. But most teams use a sliver of Datadog and pay for the whole thing, on infrastructure they don't control, with a bill that climbs every time they add a host.

If that is you, the math is simple: a flat $899/mo for unlimited self-hosted hosts versus a five-figure-per-month invoice that grows with your estate. ## Frequently asked questions

### Can you self-host Datadog?

No. Datadog is a SaaS platform — metrics, traces, APM, dashboards, and the UI run on Datadog's cloud. The only partial exception is CloudPrem, a preview feature that stores logs in your own cloud account but still requires the Datadog SaaS control plane. Pharlux is fully self-hosted: the binary, the storage, and your data all stay on your VPS.

### How much does Datadog cost compared to Pharlux?

Datadog bills per host and per usage: Infrastructure is roughly $15–23 per host per month, APM adds about $31–40 per host, and logs are billed per GB ingested plus per million events indexed. At 250 hosts, Infrastructure plus APM alone is on the order of $130,000 per year before logs. Pharlux is a flat monthly license — Community free, Team $49, Business $199, Scale $899 — regardless of host count.

### Where does my data live with Datadog vs Pharlux?

With Datadog, your telemetry lives in Datadog's cloud in the region you select; it cannot move between regions and you do not hold the raw store. With Pharlux, all telemetry is written to local Parquet on your own VPS — nothing leaves your infrastructure.

### Is Pharlux a full replacement for Datadog?

For metrics and logs on a single VPS, yes. Pharlux does not replace Datadog's full breadth — 900+ integrations, RUM, synthetics, security monitoring, and fully-managed APM. Pharlux is OpenTelemetry-native metrics and logs (traces on the roadmap) that you run and own.

### How do I move from Datadog to Pharlux?

Datadog supports OpenTelemetry ingestion, so if you already run an OpenTelemetry Collector you add an exporter pointing at Pharlux on `:4318` and run both in parallel, then wind down Datadog once Pharlux covers what you need.
