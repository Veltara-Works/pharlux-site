# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [set at launch]_

This policy explains how **Veltara Works** (ABN 49 625 284 330) ("we", "us") handles personal information collected through the Pharlux website at pharlux.com. Pharlux is a product of Veltara Works. We keep this deliberately short because we collect very little.

## The short version

- We run **no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies** on this website.
- We only collect personal data when **you send it to us** — through the contact form or when you buy a plan.
- The **Pharlux software is self-hosted and never phones home**: your metrics, logs, and other telemetry stay on your own machine and never reach us. There is no activation server, usage beacon, or telemetry leaving your box.

## What we collect and why

**Contact form.** When you use our [contact form](/contact), we collect the name, email address, message, and enquiry type/tier you provide, so we can respond to you. The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile (anti-spam), which may process your IP address and browser signals to tell humans from bots.

**Purchases.** When self-serve purchasing is available and you buy a plan, our payment processor **Stripe** handles your payment. Card details are entered directly with Stripe — **we never see or store your full card number**. We receive and store the information we need to provide and bill your subscription and issue your license: your name, email, billing country, and subscription and license details.

**Website hosting.** The site is served by **Cloudflare**, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses and browser type in server logs to deliver and secure the site.

**Your browser.** The site stores your light/dark theme preference locally in your browser. That preference stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## How we use your information

To respond to your enquiries, to provide and bill your subscription and manage your license, to keep the site and our services secure, and to meet our legal obligations. We do **not** sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers that help us run Pharlux:

- **Cloudflare** — website hosting, content delivery, and bot protection (Turnstile).
- **[Vectis Mail](https://vectismail.com)** (our own email service) — to deliver and store contact-form enquiries and transactional email such as receipts and license delivery.
- **Stripe** — payment processing and subscription management (when self-serve purchasing launches).

We may also disclose information where required by law.

## International transfers

Some of the service providers we use, such as Cloudflare and Stripe, operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where we transfer data, we take reasonable steps to ensure it stays protected.

## How long we keep it

We keep contact-form enquiries and customer and billing records for as long as needed to respond to you, provide the service, and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations, then delete or anonymise them.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. No system is perfectly secure, but we design for minimal collection — the less we hold, the less is ever at risk.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to stop using it for a given purpose. We handle personal information in line with the **Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles**. If you're in the EEA or UK, you also have rights under the **GDPR / UK GDPR**. To exercise any of these, contact us below.

If you're in Australia and unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

## Children

Pharlux is a product for businesses and developers. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

## Changes

We may update this policy. If we make a material change, we'll update the "last updated" date above.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the [contact form](/contact) and we'll route it to the right place.
